# Section Introduction

This walkthrough demonstrates the high-fidelity interface flow and interaction rationale using frontend capture states and mock interaction data.

# Function Descriptions

## Album / Connect

Album / Connect now opens through a member overview and then moves into a per-member photo stream. The updated flow emphasizes lightweight browsing, time-range filtering, quick reactions, and a simple self-upload entry point.

## Jar

Jar now combines private mood logging with selective sharing. The updated flow highlights the mood-candy metaphor, the private calendar, visibility controls, and the separate shared-status care view.

## Weekly Echo

Weekly Echo now unfolds as a weekly recap sequence. The updated flow moves from summary, to small moments, to shared keepsakes, and ends in a dedicated reward reveal scene.

# Tooltip Copy

## Album / Connect

- Open a family member's update stream: Choose whose recent photo updates to view from the Album overview.
- Browse one shared moment without entering chat: Read the photo, timestamp, and note together as a lightweight update.
- Shift the gallery time window: Narrow the visible stream to the period that matters now.
- Respond with a low-pressure reaction: Acknowledge the moment through a quick emoji rather than a full reply.
- Start a self-upload in one step: Open the Camera or Library chooser directly from the current user's album.

## Jar

- Start today's mood check-in: Add one mood candy from a single entry point.
- Review moods through a private calendar: Revisit recorded days without exposing the full history to family.
- Set visibility before saving: Decide whether the mood stays private, softly shared, or fully shared.
- Expose only shared status in care view: Relatives see only the layer the user chose to share.

## Weekly Echo

- Read the weekly summary board: View the week's connected days, small moments, and activity counts together.
- Scan the small-moments recap: Read the week back as a short, low-pressure narrative.
- Review what the pet kept from the week: See how small traces are grouped into shared keepsakes.
- Open the keepsake reveal scene: Transition from the board view into the weekly reward state.
- Claim the unlocked keepsake: Add the revealed item to the toy box from the same scene.

# Step Captions

- Album / Connect: Member overview, gallery detail, time filter, reaction tray, upload entry.
- Jar: Entry point, private calendar, share-mode editor, shared-status care popup.
- Weekly Echo: Summary board, moments board, keepsakes board, gift reveal, reward claim.
