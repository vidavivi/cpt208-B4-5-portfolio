Switch between the three core interaction flows and preview grouped screenshots with matching design explanations.

🌤️ Lightweight Sharing

A low-effort way to share daily moments without starting a full conversation.

  • User action: Upload or view a small family moment, then respond with a quick reply or light reaction.
  • Design value: Keeps acknowledgement visible but avoids forcing a long chat.
  • Requirement supported: Low Reply Pressure.

🌷 Gentle Topics

A prompt-based entry point for starting family conversations more easily.

  • User action: Browse, refresh, and send a topic card to a family member.
  • Design value: Reduces the burden of thinking of what to say from scratch.
  • Requirement supported: Easy to Start.

🫙 Private Mood Jar

A private mood-reflection flow with limited, abstract family awareness.

  • User action: Record a mood by placing a mood item into the jar and review the private mood log.
  • Design value: Supports self-reflection while avoiding direct family monitoring.
  • Requirement supported: Flexible Timing and emotional privacy.
