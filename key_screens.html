🌤️ Lightweight Sharing

A gentle way to say “this is a little piece of my day.”

Users can share a photo or short video with only a few words, turning daily life into something soft, visible, and easy to send.

💬 Instead of asking for a full conversation, the screen invites quick replies, light reactions, and small moments of response that feel effortless but still warm.

✨ The experience is designed to feel casual, low-pressure, and emotionally close — more like leaving a trace of your day than starting a serious chat.

📱 Home UI Interaction

1. Tap Upload to post a new daily moment through a photo or short video.

2. View a family member’s shared post directly in the feed.

3. Choose a quick reply for low-effort interaction, or tap Add reply to type a short custom response.

4. After replying, the interaction remains light and informal, helping communication continue without pressure.

🌷 Gentle Topics

The page offers gentle prompts that help family members start talking without the pressure of thinking of the perfect message.

💌 Users can send a topic card directly, open family invitations from the top reminder card, or choose who to invite through the invite action.

✨ Instead of forcing conversation, this feature creates a warm entry point — light enough for everyday use, but meaningful enough to support emotional connection.

💡 Connect UI Interaction

1. Browse the topic cards shown on the page and choose one that feels easy to share.

2. Tap Send to turn the prompt into a conversation invitation.

3. Tap the invite icon to choose which family member to send it to.

4. Tap the notification card to review incoming invitations, then accept or decline them.

5. Tap refresh to receive a new batch of gentle topics and keep the interaction feeling fresh.

🫙 Private Mood Jar

It acts as a gentle mirror of the family’s overall emotional atmosphere.

📅 Users record their daily mood by throwing a candy into the jar. Over time, the jar fills with coloured mood drops and the private calendar builds a gentle visual history of everyday emotional change.

🐾 The pet and mood jar reflect the overall family atmosphere, while the mood log itself remains private and only visible to the user, preserving emotional space and personal control.

💡 Mood Jar UI Interaction

1. View the pet’s current state as a soft reflection of the recent family atmosphere.

2. Tap Throw a candy for today to start recording your mood.

3. Choose one emotion from the mood popup and optionally add a short personal note. Save the entry to drop a coloured mood candy into the jar.

5. Open the private mood log to review your emotional calendar over time.

6. Use the pet selection page to choose the companion you prefer.
